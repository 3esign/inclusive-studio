Architecture / Year 03 / Pilot proposal

An everyday place. A shared possibility.

Work at three connected scales: the journey to a place, the organisation of that place, and one detail that changes its use. Credits, assessment and workload must be agreed with the course team.

  1. Trace a journey
  2. Agree the brief
  3. Compare spatial options
  4. Test at full scale
  5. Resolve the project
  6. Return it to the community

Svakodnevno mesto. Zajednička mogućnost.

Radite u tri povezane razmere: put do mesta, organizacija mesta i detalj koji menja korišćenje. Bodove, ocenjivanje i opterećenje treba dogovoriti sa nastavnim timom.
