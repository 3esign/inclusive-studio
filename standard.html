1 · Svrha: tri funkcije obrazovanja1 · Purpose: three functions of education

Obrazovanje, po Gertu Biesti, uvek radi tri posla odjednom: kvalifikaciju (znanje i veštine), socijalizaciju (uvođenje u zajednicu prakse) i subjektifikaciju (postajanje osobom koja ume da započne nešto novo). Doba merenja meri samo prvo i time tiho briše ostala dva — ovaj studio imenuje sva tri i ne žrtvuje nijedno (Biesta 2009). Education, after Gert Biesta, always does three jobs at once: qualification (knowledge and skills), socialisation (induction into a community of practice) and subjectification (becoming a person able to begin something new). The age of measurement measures only the first and thereby quietly erases the other two — this studio names all three and sacrifices none (Biesta 2009).

KvalifikacijaQualification

Merljivi ishodi učenja na nivou EQF 6, konstruktivno poravnati sa zadacima i ocenjivanjem — vidi Program. Measurable learning outcomes at EQF level 6, constructively aligned with tasks and assessment — see the Programme.

SocijalizacijaSocialisation

Studio je zajednica prakse u kojoj su i korisnici punopravni članovi — treća misija univerziteta izvedena, ne deklarisana (Boyer 1996). The studio is a community of practice in which users are full members — the university's third mission practised, not declared (Boyer 1996).

SubjektifikacijaSubjectification

I student i član udruženja izlaze iz studija sa sopstvenim glasom: student ume da brani odluku pred stvarnim korisnikom, korisnik je autor čije ime stoji na radu. Both student and association member leave the studio with a voice of their own: the student can defend a decision before a real user, the user is an author whose name stands on the work.

2 · Nasleđe na kome stojimo2 · The inheritance we stand on

  • Humbolt (1809/10): nastava i istraživanje su nerazdvojivi — profesor i student su saradnici u traganju. Studio je upravo to: zajedničko istraživanje, ne prenos gradiva. Humboldt (1809/10): teaching and research are inseparable — professor and student are companions in inquiry. The studio is exactly that: joint research, not transmission of content.
  • Djui (1916): demokratija je „način udruženog življenja" i uči se praksom; znanje nastaje rešavanjem stvarnih problema (Democracy and Education). Dewey (1916): democracy is "a mode of associated living" learned by practice; knowledge arises from solving real problems (Democracy and Education).
  • Vajthed (1929): znanje koje se ne upotrebi je „inertna ideja" — štetno, ne samo beskorisno. Ovde se svako znanje u roku od nedelju dana primeni na stvaran prostor. Whitehead (1929): knowledge left unused is an "inert idea" — harmful, not merely useless. Here every piece of knowledge is applied to a real place within a week.
  • Freire (1970): protiv „bankarskog modela" — oni o čijim se životima radi moraju biti subjekti istraživanja, ne objekti (Pedagogy of the Oppressed). Freire (1970): against the "banking model" — those whose lives are at stake must be subjects of the inquiry, not its objects (Pedagogy of the Oppressed).
  • bel huks (1994): angažovana pedagogija — različitost učesnika je pedagoški resurs, a glas i bezbednost svakog učesnika su uslov učenja. bell hooks (1994): engaged pedagogy — the diversity of participants is a pedagogical resource, and every participant's voice and safety are a condition of learning.

The context that makes these norms urgent: about 19% of students in Europe report limitations in their studies due to disability or a health problem (EUROSTUDENT 8, 2024) — and those students more often report obstacles and a weaker sense of belonging.

4 · Okvir struke: šta obrazovanje arhitekte mora da pruži4 · The profession's frame: what architectural education must provide

EU Direktiva 2005/36/EC, čl. 46 propisuje 11 tačaka znanja za arhitekte. Ovaj studio direktno vežba četiri: (e) razumevanje odnosa ljudi i zgrada i ljudske mere, (f) ulogu arhitekte u društvu i projektne zadatke koji računaju sa socijalnim faktorima, (g) metode istraživanja i pripreme projektnog zadatka, i (j) projektovanje koje zadovoljava zahteve korisnika u granicama propisa. EU Directive 2005/36/EC, art. 46 prescribes 11 points of knowledge for architects. This studio directly exercises four: (e) understanding the relations between people and buildings and human scale, (f) the architect's role in society and briefs that account for social factors, (g) methods of investigation and brief preparation, and (j) design that meets users' requirements within the constraints of regulations.

  • UNESCO/UIA povelja o arhitektonskom obrazovanju (izdanje 2026) traži obrazovanje koje odgovara na potrebe svih članova društva — i od 2026. sadrži smernice o AI u nastavi: AI pojačava, ne zamenjuje projektantsko mišljenje. The UNESCO/UIA Charter for Architectural Education (2026 edition) demands education that answers the needs of all members of society — and since 2026 includes guidance on AI in teaching: AI amplifies, it does not replace, design thinking.
  • U američkoj akreditaciji pristupačnost je obavezni deo projektne sinteze (NAAB 2020, kriterijum SC.5 „Design Synthesis" i PC.8 „Social Equity and Inclusive Environments"); u britanskoj je „Inclusive Environments" obavezna tema (RIBA). In US accreditation, accessibility is a mandatory part of design synthesis (NAAB 2020, criterion SC.5 "Design Synthesis" and PC.8 "Social Equity and Inclusive Environments"); in the UK, "Inclusive Environments" is a mandatory theme (RIBA).
  • Srpski akreditacioni standardi (NAT/KAPK) su generički i ne traže univerzalni dizajn kao ishod učenja arhitekture — taj jaz je upravo prostor koji ovaj studio popunjava. Najveća anketa o UD u nastavi arhitekture (IDeA Center, 120 škola) nalazi: najdelotvornije metode uključuju osobe sa invaliditetom u samu nastavu. Serbian accreditation standards (NAT/KAPK) are generic and do not require universal design as a learning outcome in architecture — that gap is precisely the space this studio fills. The largest survey of UD in architectural teaching (IDeA Center, 120 schools) finds: the most effective methods bring persons with disabilities into the teaching itself.

5 · Standard kvaliteta kursa5 · Course quality standard

Studio se drži evropskog standarda dizajna kursa — i to se može proveriti na strani Program: The studio follows the European standard of course design — verifiable on the Programme page:

  • ECTS računica: 1 ECTS = 25–30 sati ukupnog rada; opterećenje je razloženo po aktivnostima i javno (ECTS Users' Guide 2015). ECTS arithmetic: 1 ECTS = 25–30 hours of total work; workload is broken down by activity and public (ECTS Users' Guide 2015).
  • Merljivi ishodi na jeziku nivoa 6 Evropskog okvira kvalifikacija, 1–2 glagola po ishodu, usklađeni sa Dablinskim deskriptorima. Measurable outcomes in EQF level-6 language, 1–2 verbs per outcome, aligned with the Dublin descriptors.
  • Konstruktivno poravnanje (Biggs): svaka aktivnost vežba ishode, svako ocenjivanje meri ishode; kriterijumi su objavljeni unapred, binarno, po modelu specifications grading (Nilson). Constructive alignment (Biggs): every activity exercises the outcomes, every assessment measures them; criteria are published in advance, binary, on the specifications-grading model (Nilson).
  • Studentocentrična nastava i petlja kvaliteta (ESG 2015, standardi 1.3 i 1.9): kriterijumi unapred, pravo na doradu, a posle svakog ciklusa revizija programa na osnovu povratnih informacija učesnika. Student-centred teaching and a quality loop (ESG 2015, standards 1.3 and 1.9): criteria up front, a right to revise, and after each cycle a programme review based on participants' feedback.
  • Univerzalni dizajn učenja (CAST): informacije o prilagođavanjima na vrhu programa, materijali u više formata, opcije u načinu demonstracije ishoda (tekst, crtež, govor), predvidljiv nedeljni ritam. Universal Design for Learning (CAST): accommodation information at the top of the programme, materials in multiple formats, options in how outcomes are demonstrated (text, drawing, speech), a predictable weekly rhythm.

6 · Šta izričito odbijamo6 · What we explicitly refuse

  • Studenta-potrošača. Potrošačka orijentacija empirijski snižava učenje (Bunce i dr. 2017); uspeh studija se ne meri „zadovoljstvom uslugom" nego učenjem, doprinosom zajednici i subjektifikacijom. The student-as-consumer. Consumer orientation empirically lowers learning (Bunce et al. 2017); the studio's success is measured not by "service satisfaction" but by learning, contribution to the community, and subjectification.
  • Projektovanje za žiri. Klasična kritika pred panelom uči studente da performiraju za kritičare umesto da brane korisnika (Webster 2006; Anthony, Design Juries on Trial, 1991). Ovde finalnu reč ima korisnik, a kritika je dijaloška i formativna. Designing for the jury. The classic panel crit teaches students to perform for critics instead of defending the user (Webster 2006; Anthony, Design Juries on Trial, 1991). Here the user has the final word, and critique is dialogic and formative.
  • Simulacije invaliditeta. Kratke simulacije (povez, kolica „na probu") merljivo pogoršavaju stavove prema osobama sa invaliditetom; zamenjuje ih stvaran, plaćen ili priznat rad sa ljudima — vidi Etiku. Disability simulations. Short simulations (blindfolds, "trying out" a wheelchair) measurably worsen attitudes toward disabled people; they are replaced by real, paid or credited collaboration with people — see Ethics.
  • Studio-kulturu nespavanja. Kultura prekovremenog rada i „svemoćnog studija" dokumentovano šteti učenju (AIAS 2002); opterećenje ovog studija je ograničeno ECTS računicom i poštuje se. The sleepless studio culture. The culture of overwork and the "almighty studio" demonstrably harms learning (AIAS 2002); this studio's workload is capped by the ECTS arithmetic and the cap is respected.

Naše odluke bez spoljnog izvora: šest nedelja umesto semestra (pilot mora biti izvodljiv uz postojeće obaveze), GitHub kao platforma (javnost i trajnost, uz dokumentovane mane — vidi Pristupačnost), i dvojezičnost sajta (srpski i engleski ravnopravno). Our decisions without an external source: six weeks instead of a semester (a pilot must be feasible alongside existing obligations), GitHub as the platform (publicness and permanence, with documented flaws — see Accessibility), and the site's bilingualism (Serbian and English as equals).
