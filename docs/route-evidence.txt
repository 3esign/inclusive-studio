For each route segment record:

2. Clear width + unit + method, or NOT MEASURED
3. Change in level / slope + unit + method, or NOT MEASURED
4. Surface, crossings, doors, obstacles and turning/pausing space
5. Light, glare, noise, shade and places to rest
6. Observation date, conditions and uncertainty

ALTERNATIVE A / ALTERNATIVE B
What activity does each enable? For whom and under which conditions?
What additional effort or assistance does each require?
What remains unknown? What must be checked on site?

SHARED DECISION
Keep / revise / try next:
Who disagreed and what changed? Use roles or agreed pseudonyms.
How will the change be tested, by whom, and with what stop condition?

DRAWING COMPANION
Orientation, units, scale, key spaces, route sequence, critical dimensions,
and the design decision the drawing supports. Do not infer accessibility
from a photograph or a dimension without context.
