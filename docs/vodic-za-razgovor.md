# Vodič za razgovor (Nedelja 2) · Interview guide (Week 2)

## Pre razgovora / Before
- Dogovorite mesto koje je pristupačno sagovorniku — pitaj, ne pretpostavljaj. / Agree on a place accessible to your partner — ask, don't assume.
- Objasni čemu razgovor služi i gde će beleške završiti (javna predaja na platformi, uz saglasnost). / Explain the purpose and where notes will end up (public submission, with consent).

## Pravila / Rules
1. Osoba vodi, ti slušaš. / The person leads, you listen.
2. Pitaš o **putanjama i prostorima**, ne o dijagnozi. / Ask about **routes and spaces**, not diagnoses.
3. Svako pitanje sme da se preskoči. / Any question may be skipped.
4. Beleške pokaži sagovorniku pre predaje. / Show your notes to your partner before submitting.

## Pitanja / Questions
- Opiši mi svoj običan dan kroz grad — od vrata do vrata. / Walk me through an ordinary day across the city — door to door.
- Gde staneš? Gde zaobilaziš? Gde si odustao/la? / Where do you stop? Where do you detour? Where have you given up?
- Koji prostor je **dobro** rešen — i zašto baš on? / Which space is done **well** — and why that one?
- Šta su ljudi koji projektuju najčešće pogrešno pretpostavili o tebi? / What do designers most often wrongly assume about you?
- Kad bi sutra mogao/la da promeniš jedan prostor, koji bi to bio? / If you could change one space tomorrow, which would it be?

## Posle / After
Zajedno izdvojte 3–5 barijera i unesite ih kao mapu barijera (tekst ili skica) kroz predaju za nedelju 2. / Together pick 3–5 barriers and submit them as a barrier map (text or sketch) via the week 2 submission.
