Week 1 / Day one / By hand

An application for one person.

Not an app for a group, not an app for a diagnosis. One person you have watched in one situation, and the first three screens of something that does one thing for them.

What is handed in

  1. One A3: three screens at real size, about 7 × 15 cm each. Draw by hand, or use an equivalent digital, tactile, collage or assisted medium with the same scale and explanation.
  2. A purpose written next to every element. An element with no purpose is removed.
  3. The person and the observed situation — where, when, what you saw.
  4. The constraint card you drew, and how the design obeys it.
  5. One sentence: what the application refuses to do.
  6. Three questions for the person.

When it passes

  • An invented user fails. Write where and when you observed the situation.
  • An application that does everything fails. The refusal is part of the design.
  • A screen that only works as a picture fails: say it in one line and it must still hold.
  • “An app for the blind” fails. One person, one situation, one thing.

The physical A3 is reviewed in class and signed by the teacher on paper. The optional sketchpad on this page needs JavaScript and is not a submission route.
