UčestvujParticipate

Tri koraka: nalog, forma, predaja. Tekst, fotografija, skica ili snimak — svaki format vredi jednako. Three steps: account, form, submission. Text, photo, sketch or recording — every format counts equally.

Kako se učestvujeHow to take part

1 · NalogAccount

Napravi besplatan nalog na github.com/signup. Treba ti samo e-adresa. Nalog je tvoj identitet na platformi — sve što objaviš nosi tvoje ime. Create a free account at github.com/signup. You only need an e-mail address. The account is your identity on the platform — everything you publish carries your name.

2 · FormaForm

Ideje i predaje idu kroz prave web forme sa označenim poljima (pristupačnije od praznog polja za tekst): 💡 Nova ideja ili dugme „Predaj" uz svaku nedelju u Programu. Ideas and submissions go through proper web forms with labelled fields (more accessible than a blank text box): 💡 New idea, or the "Submit" button next to each week in the Programme.

3 · PrilogAttachment

Sliku ili skicu prevuci u polje za tekst — platforma je sama okači. Uz svaku sliku napiši jednu rečenicu šta prikazuje (to je njen tekstualni opis). Drag an image or sketch into the text field — the platform attaches it automatically. With every image, write one sentence saying what it shows (that is its text description).

Zapeo/la si ili ti platforma pravi barijeru? Javi se voditelju radionice uživo, ili otvori običnu prijavu sa jednom rečenicom — pomoći ćemo i prilagoditi zadatak. Barijera na samoj platformi je istovremeno i ideja za tablu. Stuck, or does the platform itself create a barrier? Tell the workshop lead in person, or open a plain issue with a single sentence — we will help and adapt the task. A barrier on the platform is simultaneously an idea for the board.

SkicirnicaSketchpad

Nacrtaj ideju prstom ili mišem, preuzmi je kao sliku, pa je prevuci u formu. Skica je jedan od ravnopravnih formata, ne obavezan: tekst, govor pretvoren u tekst ili fotografija papirne skice vrede jednako. Crtež na ekranu nije dostupan čitačima ekrana — zato svaka skica u predaji nosi i rečenicu-dve opisa, a ceo zadatak se uvek može predati i čistim tekstom. Draw an idea with a finger or mouse, download it as an image, then drag it into the form. A sketch is one of several equal formats, not mandatory: text, speech-to-text, or a photo of a paper sketch count just as much. An on-screen drawing is not available to screen readers — so every sketch in a submission also carries a sentence or two of description, and any task can always be submitted as plain text.
