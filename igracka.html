Week 1 · Day one · By hand

A toy that teaches space.

The association “Živimo zajedno” builds its own toys out of a cardboard carton, bamboo skewers and plastic bottle caps. Design the next one: it must train a movement of the hand and an operation on space at the same time, and it must be buildable in one afternoon from what a household throws away. One A3, drawn by hand, reviewed and signed in the room.

  • This week and the three notices
  • The task in the exercise bank
  • By age: the limits, the law and what a child of that age can do
  • Creative hub: literature, cases and open questions
  • Laboratory
  • The A3 template and all exercises

Safety rule carried on the sheet: no loose part may fit entirely inside the small-parts cylinder of ⌀31,7 mm (depth 25,4–57,1 mm) if the toy is for a child under three — EN 71-1, Safety of toys ↗.
