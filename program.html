Program i silabusProgramme and syllabus

Pun silabus pilota: ishodi, opterećenje, poravnanje, nedelje sa javnim kriterijumima predaje, ocenjivanje i politika upotrebe AI. Ništa se ne pogađa — sve je objavljeno unapred. The pilot's full syllabus: outcomes, workload, alignment, weeks with public submission criteria, assessment and the AI policy. Nothing is guessed — everything is published up front.

Pristupačnost učešća — prvo, ne poslednjeAccessibility of participation — first, not last

Svaki zadatak u programu ima ravnopravnu alternativu formata: gde piše „fotografiši" važi i „opiši"; gde piše „nacrtaj" važi i „izdiktiraj" ili „opiši tekstom"; gde piše „prezentuj" važi i unapred snimljen ili pisani oblik. Ako ti bilo koji deo programa pravi barijeru, javi se pre početka ili bilo kad tokom rada — kontakt — i prilagodićemo zadatak, ne tebe. Ovo je na vrhu dokumenta namerno (praksa univerzalnog dizajna učenja, CAST). Every task in the programme has an equal-format alternative: where it says "photograph", "describe" counts too; where it says "draw", "dictate" or "describe in text" counts; where it says "present", a pre-recorded or written form counts. If any part of the programme creates a barrier for you, say so before the start or at any point — contact — and we will adapt the task, not you. This sits at the top of the document on purpose (Universal Design for Learning practice, CAST).

Ishodi učenjaLearning outcomes

Posle programa student ume da:After the programme, a student is able to:

  1. proceni postojeći javni prostor prema 7 principa univerzalnog dizajna i važećim propisima pristupačnosti, dokumentujući barijere fotografijom, opisom i merenjem; evaluate an existing public space against the 7 principles of universal design and applicable accessibility regulations, documenting barriers by photograph, description and measurement;
  2. sprovede istraživanje sa korisnicima — intervju i zajedničku šetnju — po etičkom protokolu programa, bez svođenja osobe na dijagnozu; conduct research with users — an interview and a joint walk — under the programme's ethical protocol, without reducing a person to a diagnosis;
  3. prevede nalaze istraživanja u projektni zadatak sa proverljivim zahtevima (svaki zahtev ima meru ili test); translate research findings into a brief with verifiable requirements (each requirement has a measure or a test);
  4. sintetiše arhitektonsko rešenje (osnova, presek, detalj) koje integriše zahteve pristupačnosti bez segregacije korisnika — jedno rešenje za sve, ne „poseban ulaz"; synthesise an architectural solution (plan, section, detail) that integrates accessibility requirements without segregating users — one solution for everyone, not a "separate entrance";
  5. obrazloži projektne odluke pred korisnicima i stručnom publikom i kritički reflektuje sopstveni proces učenja. justify design decisions before users and a professional audience and critically reflect on their own learning process.

Član udruženja koji to želi stiče i sopstvene ishode: artikuliše prostorne barijere kao proverljive zahteve i recenzira projektna rešenja — veštine zastupanja koje ostaju i posle pilota. An association member who wishes to also gains outcomes of their own: to articulate spatial barriers as verifiable requirements and to review design proposals — advocacy skills that remain after the pilot.

Ishodi su pisani jezikom EQF nivoa 6 i pokrivaju svih pet dimenzija Dablinskih deskriptora; glagoli su sa viših nivoa Blumove taksonomije (procena, sinteza, refleksija). Direktno vežbaju tačke (e), (f), (g) i (j) čl. 46 EU Direktive 2005/36/EC — vidi Standard §4. Outcomes are written in EQF level-6 language and cover all five Dublin-descriptor dimensions; the verbs come from the upper levels of Bloom's taxonomy (evaluation, synthesis, reflection). They directly exercise points (e), (f), (g) and (j) of art. 46 of EU Directive 2005/36/EC — see Standard §4.

Plafon je stvaran: program je projektovan da stane u ovih 78 sati i ne oslanja se na noćni rad — vidi šta odbijamo. Posle pilota opterećenje se proverava anketom učesnika (ECTS vodič traži upravo to). The cap is real: the programme is designed to fit within these 78 hours and does not rely on all-nighters — see what we refuse. After the pilot, the workload is checked by a participant survey (exactly what the ECTS Guide requires).

Šest nedeljaSix weeks

Svaka nedelja nosi: cilj, zadatak za studente, ulogu za članove udruženja, predaju i javne kriterijume — binarne, objavljene unapred (specifications grading). Predaja koja ne ispuni kriterijume se ne ocenjuje negativno nego se vraća na doradu (jedna dorada po predaji). Each week carries: a goal, a task for students, a role for association members, a deliverable and public criteria — binary, published in advance (specifications grading). A submission that misses the criteria is not marked down but returned for revision (one revision per submission).

  1. Nedelja 1 — PosmatrajWeek 1 — Observe · ishod 1outcome 1

    Cilj: razumeti 7 principa univerzalnog dizajna i socijalni model invaliditeta — barijera je u prostoru, ne u osobi.Goal: understand the 7 principles of universal design and the social model of disability — the barrier is in the space, not in the person.

    Studenti: izaberi jedan javni objekat koji dobro poznaješ i prođi ga sa ček-listom 7 principa. Dokumentuj 5 mesta gde dizajn isključuje (fotografija ili opis).Students: pick a public building you know well and walk it with the 7-principles checklist. Document 5 places where the design excludes (photo or description).

    Članovi udruženja: opiši jedan prostor u gradu koji ti pravi najviše problema — i jedan koji je dobro rešen.Association members: describe one place in the city that gives you the most trouble — and one that is well solved.

    Kriterijumi predaje (svi moraju biti ispunjeni):Submission criteria (all must be met):

    • 5 dokumentovanih mesta, svako sa po jednom rečenicom: koji princip UD je narušen i koga isključuje;5 documented places, each with one sentence: which UD principle is violated and whom it excludes;
    • bar 1 primer dobrog rešenja, sa razlogom zašto radi;at least 1 example of a good solution, with the reason it works;
    • svaka fotografija ima tekstualni opis (alt).every photo has a text description (alt).

    Studenti: razgovor u paru sa članom udruženja (45 min, vodič za razgovor). Pitaš o svakodnevnim putanjama, ne o dijagnozi.Students: a paired conversation with an association member (45 min, interview guide). Ask about daily routes, not diagnoses.

    Članovi udruženja: ti vodiš — tvoje iskustvo je građa. Imaš pravo da ne odgovoriš na bilo šta i da prekineš razgovor bez objašnjenja.Association members: you lead — your experience is the material. You may decline any question and end the conversation without explanation.

OcenjivanjeAssessment

  • Model: specifications grading — svaka predaja se meri samo prema svojim javnim kriterijumima, binarno (ispunjava / vraća se na doradu). Nema skrivenih očekivanja ni poena za utisak. Model: specifications grading — each submission is measured only against its public criteria, binarily (meets / returned for revision). No hidden expectations, no points for impression.
  • Dorada: svaka predaja ima pravo na jednu doradu. Dorada nije kazna nego deo metode (ESG 1.3: ocenjivanje koje omogućava učenje). Revision: every submission carries the right to one revision. Revision is not a penalty but part of the method (ESG 1.3: assessment that enables learning).
  • Uslov završetka: svih 6 predaja ispunjava kriterijume + finalna prezentacija održana pred korisnicima. Učesnik dobija javnu potvrdu sa spiskom demonstriranih ishoda. Completion condition: all 6 submissions meet their criteria + the final presentation delivered before users. Participants receive a public certificate listing the outcomes demonstrated.
  • Uloga korisničke ocene: negativan odgovor na „da li bi ovo koristio/la?" ne obara predaju — ali mora biti javno zabeležen zajedno sa studentovim odgovorom. Učimo i iz rešenja koja nisu prošla; sakriven neuspeh je jedini pravi neuspeh. The role of the user verdict: a negative answer to "would you use this?" does not fail the submission — but it must be publicly recorded together with the student's response. We learn from solutions that did not pass; a hidden failure is the only real failure.
  • Ako se izvodi u okviru predmeta: fakultet može mapirati završetak pilota na deo predispitnih obaveza; preporučeno mapiranje je u repou i otvoreno za prilagođavanje. If run inside a course: the faculty may map pilot completion onto part of the coursework; a recommended mapping lives in the repo and is open to adaptation.

Politika upotrebe AIAI use policy

Politika je izvedena iz ishoda, ne iz straha (praksa koju traže savremeni silabus-vodiči): The policy is derived from the outcomes, not from fear (the practice current syllabus guides require):

  • Dozvoljeno uz deklaraciju: generisanje varijanti, provera teksta, pomoć u crtanju i prevodu. U svakoj predaji se navede šta je rađeno uz AI i kojim alatom. Allowed with declaration: generating variants, checking text, help with drawing and translation. Each submission states what was done with AI and which tool.
  • Zabranjeno: izmišljanje merenja, generisanje „iskustva korisnika" ili zamena razgovora sa čovekom — ishodi 2 i 5 se ne mogu demonstrirati preko posrednika. AI ne može da izmeri nagib ni da ti kaže gde čovek odustaje od puta. Forbidden: fabricating measurements, generating "user experience" or replacing the conversation with a human — outcomes 2 and 5 cannot be demonstrated by proxy. AI can neither measure a slope nor tell you where a person gives up on a route.
  • Obrazloženje: isto načelo kao u UNESCO/UIA povelji 2026 — AI pojačava projektantsko mišljenje, ne zamenjuje ga. Rationale: the same principle as the UNESCO/UIA Charter 2026 — AI amplifies design thinking, it does not replace it.
