Etika učešćaEthics of participation

„Ništa o nama bez nas" ovde nije slogan nego protokol: ko odlučuje, ko potpisuje, šta se sme zabeležiti i šta učesnik dobija. "Nothing about us without us" is not a slogan here but a protocol: who decides, who signs, what may be recorded, and what a participant receives.

Ukratko (lako čitanje)In short (easy read)

  • Ti odlučuješ šta deliš. Možeš da odbiješ svako pitanje.You decide what you share. You may refuse any question.
  • Ne pitamo za dijagnoze. Pitamo za prostor.We do not ask about diagnoses. We ask about space.
  • Tvoje ime stoji na radu, ako to želiš. Ti si autor.Your name stands on the work, if you wish. You are an author.
  • Možeš da odustaneš bilo kad. Niko te neće pitati zašto.You may withdraw at any time. Nobody will ask why.

1 · Učešće, ne konsultacija1 · Participation, not consultation

Klasična lestvica participacije (Arnstein 1969) razlikuje stvarnu moć odlučivanja od njenih imitacija: informisanje i konsultovanje bez uticaja na odluku su tokenizam. Ovaj studio se obavezuje na gornje prečke lestvice: The classic ladder of participation (Arnstein 1969) separates real decision-making power from its imitations: informing and consulting without influence over the decision are tokenism. This studio commits to the upper rungs:

  • članovi udruženja biraju prostor koji se obrađuje, zajedno sa studentima;association members choose the place to be worked on, together with the students;
  • učestvuju u ko-dizajnu (nedelja 4) kao autori, ne kao ispitanici;they take part in co-design (week 4) as authors, not as research subjects;
  • recenziraju razradu (nedelja 5) sa stvarnom posledicom — koncept koji ne prođe vraća se;they review the development (week 5) with a real consequence — a concept that fails goes back;
  • daju završnu ocenu korisnosti (nedelja 6) koja ulazi u javni zapis.they give the final usefulness verdict (week 6), which enters the public record.

Provera tokenizma — ako je bilo koji odgovor „ne", učešće nije stvarno:Tokenism check — if any answer is "no", participation is not real:

  • Da li učesnik može da promeni odluku, a ne samo da je čuje?Can the participant change a decision, not merely hear it?
  • Da li je uključen od izbora teme, a ne tek na kraju?Are they included from the choice of topic, not only at the end?
  • Da li je njegovo vreme priznato (autorstvo, zahvalnica, honorar gde postoji)?Is their time recognised (authorship, credit, a fee where one exists)?
  • Da li može da kaže „ne" bez posledica?Can they say "no" without consequences?

2 · Saglasnost i podaci2 · Consent and data

  • Platforma je javna. Sve što se preda vidi ceo internet, zauvek. To se kaže unapred, pre prvog doprinosa — i zato ništa lično ne sme u predaju bez izričite saglasnosti osobe na koju se odnosi. The platform is public. Everything submitted is visible to the whole internet, permanently. This is said up front, before the first contribution — which is why nothing personal may enter a submission without the explicit consent of the person concerned.
  • Minimum podataka. Beleži se iskustvo prostora, nikad dijagnoza, istorija bolesti ni pomagalo osim ako ga osoba sama imenuje kao relevantno. Predaja sa medicinskim podacima se vraća na čišćenje. Data minimum. What is recorded is the experience of space — never a diagnosis, medical history, or an aid unless the person themselves names it as relevant. A submission containing medical data is returned for cleaning.
  • Fotografije ljudi idu na platformu samo uz saglasnost svake prepoznatljive osobe; fotografija prostora je uvek dovoljna. Photos of people go on the platform only with the consent of every recognisable person; a photo of the space is always sufficient.
  • Pravo na povlačenje. Učesnik može povući svoje doprinose u bilo kom trenutku, bez obrazloženja; brišemo ih sa platforme u roku od nedelju dana (uz napomenu da javne kopije van naše kontrole mogu postojati — i to kažemo pošteno unapred). Right to withdraw. A participant may withdraw their contributions at any time, without explanation; we remove them from the platform within a week (noting honestly, up front, that public copies beyond our control may exist).
  • Potvrda sagovornika. Mapa barijera iz razgovora (nedelja 2) objavljuje se tek kad je sagovornik pročita i potvrdi — to je kriterijum predaje, ne ljubaznost. Interviewee confirmation. The barrier map from the conversation (week 2) is published only after the interviewee has read and confirmed it — that is a submission criterion, not a courtesy.

3 · Priznanje rada3 · Recognition of work

Vreme i ekspertiza članova udruženja su rad, ne dobra volja. Istraživačka literatura o participaciji je jasna: neplaćeno „konsultovanje" je oblik ekstrakcije. U ovom pilotu bez budžeta priznanje je: koautorstvo na svakom radu u kom je osoba učestvovala (ime na predaji i panou, ako želi), javna zahvalnica udruženju, i potvrda o učešću sa ishodima — ista kakvu dobijaju studenti. Obaveza za sledeći ciklus: tražiti finansiranje koje omogućava honorare; ako ga ne bude, to se javno kaže, a ne prećuti. Association members' time and expertise are work, not goodwill. The participation literature is clear: unpaid "consulting" is a form of extraction. In this zero-budget pilot, recognition means: co-authorship of every piece a person contributed to (name on the submission and the board, if they wish), public credit to the association, and a certificate of participation with outcomes — the same one students receive. An obligation for the next cycle: seek funding that allows fees; if none is found, that is said publicly, not passed over.

4 · Granice metode4 · Limits of the method

  • Bez simulacija invaliditeta. Kratke simulacije merljivo pogoršavaju stavove (vidi Standard §6); iskustvo se uči od ljudi, ne od poveza. No disability simulations. Brief simulations measurably worsen attitudes (see Standard §6); experience is learned from people, not from blindfolds.
  • Jedan čovek nije svi ljudi. Nalaz iz jednog razgovora je iskustvo, ne statistika; u predaji se piše „sagovornik kaže", ne „osobe sa invaliditetom žele". One person is not all people. A finding from one conversation is an experience, not a statistic; submissions write "the interviewee says", not "disabled people want".
  • Teret dokazivanja je na dizajnu. Kad korisnik kaže da nešto ne radi, ne traži se od njega da to „dokaže" — traži se od rešenja da se popravi ili obrazloži. The burden of proof is on the design. When a user says something does not work, they are not asked to "prove" it — the solution is asked to improve or justify itself.
  • Sukob se rešava u javnom zapisu. Neslaganje student–korisnik se dokumentuje sa oba stanovišta na predaji; voditelj radionice moderira razgovor, ne presuđuje tiho. Conflict is resolved in the public record. A student–user disagreement is documented from both standpoints on the submission; the workshop lead moderates the conversation rather than ruling silently.
