Laboratory

Measure it, then decide.

Eight short labs. Each one tests a tool, an interface or a geometry — never a person. Nobody is blindfolded here: after disability simulations people report more empathy but also more pity, and they are not more willing to work with disabled people (Nario-Redmond et al., 2017). What works is a structured exercise with the real tool, a debrief, and above all a disabled coauthor.

  • The machine reads your page — Find one fact in a notice, hearing only what a screen reader announces. Then do it again in the version without headings, alternative text or real buttons. Compare the number of steps.
  • Without a mouse — Complete a short form using Tab, Shift+Tab, Space and Enter only. One version has a focus trap and invisible focus; the other does not.
  • One button — Operate a menu with a single switch and an automatic scan. Reorder and group the items, and watch the number of activations fall.
  • Measure the colour, measure the target — Put in the two colours from your project and read the ratio. Then set a button size and see when it stops satisfying the minimum. Zoom the sample to 400 % and look for the horizontal scrollbar.
  • Who does not get the alarm — Choose the channels for an evacuation alarm in your building. The table shows who is reached once, twice, or not at all.
  • Does the chair get there, and does it turn — A real-size plan on a 10 cm grid. Move the walls and widen the doors until the route exists, the narrowest point holds and a 150 cm circle fits where you arrive. Fewer moves is a better score.
  • 22,5 metres of height — Our site falls 22,5 m over 151 m. Choose a slope and read how long the ramp becomes, how many landings it needs and whether it fits on a plot of about 129 × 131 m. This is where the project starts.
  • Twenty seconds and a long sentence — Read an administrative notice under a countdown, then rewrite it. The counter is a design property, not a fact of nature.

The labs need JavaScript. The standards they measure against do not: WCAG 2.2 and the Serbian Pravilnik 22/2015.
