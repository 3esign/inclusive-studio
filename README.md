# Inclusive Studio — pilot

**Live site: https://3esign.github.io/inclusive-studio/**

A collaborative pilot platform where 3rd-year architecture students and members of a disability association co-design accessible spaces — six weeks, one real place, users as co-authors (not "cases").

*Srpska verzija: [README.sr.md](README.sr.md)*

## How it works
- The **website** holds the six-week plan, a live idea board and a sketchpad.
- All **ideas and weekly submissions** are GitHub Issues — text, photos and sketches (drag & drop into the form). Everything is public and open for comments.
- The idea board on the site reads live from the Issues API; no backend, no build step, zero dependencies.

## Six weeks
1. **Observe** — audit a public building with the 7 principles of universal design.
2. **Meet** — paired interview with an association member ([guide](docs/vodic-za-razgovor.md)).
3. **Measure** — joint walk; barriers become numbers.
4. **Imagine** — co-design workshop, 3 sketched ideas.
5. **Develop** — one concept at scale, reviewed by the user.
6. **Present** — usefulness judged by the users, not a jury.

## Grounding
The pedagogy follows the evidence: direct, equal collaboration with disabled people is the active ingredient of accessibility education; passive disability simulations are avoided (they measurably worsen attitudes). Models: MIT 6.811 (semester-long work with a real client), UW CSE 493E (competency-based grading).
