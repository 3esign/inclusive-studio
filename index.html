Studenti arhitekture i članovi udruženja projektuju zajedno. Architecture students and disability association members design together.

Inclusive Studio je kolaborativni studio univerzalnog dizajna za 3. godinu arhitekture: šest nedelja, stvaran prostor, stvarni korisnici kao koautori i recenzenti. Otvoren silabus, otvoren standard, otvorena platforma. Inclusive Studio is a collaborative universal design studio for 3rd-year architecture students: six weeks, a real place, real users as co-authors and reviewers. Open syllabus, open standard, open platform.

Zašto ovaj studio postojiWhy this studio exists

ZanatCraft

Student uči da izmeri barijeru, napiše proverljiv projektni zadatak i sintetiše rešenje koje služi svima — veštine koje evropski okvir obrazovanja arhitekata izričito traži. Students learn to measure a barrier, write a verifiable brief and synthesise a design that serves everyone — skills the European framework for architectural education explicitly requires.

ZajednicaCommunity

Univerzitet ima obavezu angažmana sa zajednicom — ovde se ona izvodi: udruženje donosi stvarna pitanja, studio vraća proverene odgovore. Universities have a duty of community engagement — here it is practised: the association brings real questions, the studio returns verified answers.

Subjekt, ne slučajSubject, not case

Niko ovde nije „korisnik usluge" ni „studija slučaja". Članovi udruženja su koautori sa pravom veta; studenti su istraživači, ne izvođači tuđeg zadatka. Nobody here is a "service user" or a "case study". Association members are co-authors with a right of veto; students are researchers, not executors of someone else's task.

Ceo temelj — od Humbolta i Djuija do Bolonjske socijalne dimenzije i Konvencije UN o pravima osoba sa invaliditetom — ispisan je na strani Standard i svrha. The full foundation — from Humboldt and Dewey to the Bologna social dimension and the UN CRPD — is written out on the Standard & purpose page.

Kako radiHow it works

1 · Pridruži seJoin

Napravi besplatan GitHub nalog. Sve predaje, ideje i skice idu kroz ovu platformu — javno, sa imenom autora. Uputstvo je na strani Učestvuj. Create a free GitHub account. All submissions, ideas and sketches go through this platform — public, with the author's name. Instructions are on the Participate page.

2 · Radi po programuFollow the programme

Šest nedelja: Posmatraj → Upoznaj → Izmeri → Zamisli → Razradi → Pokaži. Svaka nedelja ima ishode, zadatak, ulogu za članove udruženja i javne kriterijume predaje. Six weeks: Observe → Meet → Measure → Imagine → Develop → Present. Each week has outcomes, a task, a role for association members and public submission criteria.

3 · Deli i recenzirajShare and review

Svaka ideja i predaja je otvorena za komentare. Koncept koji ne prođe proveru korisnika vraća se na doradu — to je pravilo programa, ne učtivost. Every idea and submission is open for comments. A concept that fails the user's check goes back for rework — that is a programme rule, not a courtesy.

Bez simulacija invaliditeta „na pola sata" — merenja pokazuju da pogoršavaju stavove. Aktivni sastojak je direktan, ravnopravan rad sa ljudima. Zato je svaka nedelja građena oko stvarnog susreta, a ne oko poveza preko očiju. No half-hour "disability simulations" — evidence shows they worsen attitudes. The active ingredient is direct, equal collaboration with people. That is why every week is built around a real encounter, not a blindfold.
