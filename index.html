Teacher · course flow

The flow.

Prepared is not held. Held without recorded results is not cemented. An idea carries no week until it becomes a pilot. Every item names its source and date — this is the window the course is steered from.

Protocol

Trying the prototypes at the association

A safety gate before any hands (small parts, edges); “other hands” without explanation; per user, what is measured is measured; no simulation, no judging of persons, no publishing without consent.

Waiting for: the date and the space — to be agreed with the association.
