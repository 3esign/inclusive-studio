Studenti arhitekture i članovi udruženja projektuju zajedno. Architecture students and disability association members design together.

Šestonedeljni pilot univerzalnog dizajna za 3. godinu arhitekture. Pravilo broj jedan: ništa o nama bez nas — korisnici nisu „slučaj", nego koautori. A six-week universal design pilot for 3rd-year architecture students. Rule number one: nothing about us without us — users are co-authors, not "cases".

Kako radiHow it works

1 · Pridruži seJoin

Napravi besplatan GitHub nalog. Sve predaje, ideje i skice idu kroz ovu platformu — javno, sa imenom autora. Create a free GitHub account. All submissions, ideas and sketches go through this platform — public, with the author's name.

2 · Radi nedeljne zadatkeDo the weekly tasks

Svaka nedelja ima cilj, zadatak za studente, ulogu za članove udruženja i jasnu predaju. Predaješ tekst, fotografije ili skicu — dugmetom na dnu svake nedelje. Each week has a goal, a task for students, a role for association members and a clear deliverable. You submit text, photos or a sketch — via the button under each week.

3 · Deli i komentarišiShare and discuss

Svaka ideja i predaja je otvorena za komentare. Najbolje ideje rastu kroz razgovor studenata i korisnika. Every idea and submission is open for comments. The best ideas grow through conversation between students and users.

Bez simulacija invaliditeta „na pola sata" — merenja pokazuju da pogoršavaju stavove. Aktivni sastojak je direktan, ravnopravan rad sa ljudima. No half-hour "disability simulations" — evidence shows they worsen attitudes. The active ingredient is direct, equal collaboration with people.

Šest nedeljaSix weeks

  1. Nedelja 1 — PosmatrajWeek 1 — Observe

    Cilj: razumeti 7 principa univerzalnog dizajna i socijalni model invaliditeta.Goal: understand the 7 principles of universal design and the social model of disability.

    Studenti: izaberi jedan javni objekat koji dobro poznaješ i prođi ga sa ček-listom 7 principa. Fotografiši 5 mesta gde dizajn isključuje.Students: pick a public building you know well and walk it with the 7-principles checklist. Photograph 5 places where the design excludes.

    Članovi udruženja: opiši jedan prostor u gradu koji ti pravi najviše problema — i jedan koji je dobro rešen.Association members: describe one place in the city that gives you the most trouble — and one that is well solved.

    Predaja: fotografije + kratke beleške.Deliverable: photos + short notes.

    Predaj za nedelju 1Submit for week 1

  2. Nedelja 2 — UpoznajWeek 2 — Meet

    Cilj: naučiti da slušaš pre nego što projektuješ.Goal: learn to listen before you design.

    Studenti: razgovor u paru sa članom udruženja (45 min, vodič za razgovor je u repou). Pitaš o svakodnevnim putanjama, ne o dijagnozi.Students: a paired conversation with an association member (45 min, interview guide is in the repo). Ask about daily routes, not diagnoses.

    Članovi udruženja: ti vodiš — tvoje iskustvo je građa. Imaš pravo da ne odgovoriš na bilo šta.Association members: you lead — your experience is the material. You may decline any question.

    Predaja: mapa barijera iz razgovora (tekst ili skica).Deliverable: a barrier map from the conversation (text or sketch).

    Predaj za nedelju 2Submit for week 2

  3. Nedelja 3 — IzmeriWeek 3 — Measure

    Cilj: barijera postaje broj: nagib, širina, visina, svetlo, zvuk.Goal: a barrier becomes a number: slope, width, height, light, sound.

    Studenti: zajednička šetnja sa korisnikom kroz izabrani prostor. Meri sve što korisnik označi kao prepreku.Students: a joint walk with the user through the chosen space. Measure everything the user flags as an obstacle.

    Članovi udruženja: vodič kroz sopstvenu putanju — pokaži gde staje, gde zaobilaziš, gde odustaješ.Association members: guide your own route — show where it stops you, where you detour, where you give up.

    Predaja: crtež postojećeg stanja sa ucrtanim barijerama i merama.Deliverable: an as-built drawing with barriers and measurements marked.

    Predaj za nedelju 3Submit for week 3

  4. Nedelja 4 — ZamisliWeek 4 — Imagine

    Cilj: ko-dizajn: ideje nastaju za stolom za kojim sede i student i korisnik.Goal: co-design: ideas are born at a table where both student and user sit.

    Studenti i članovi zajedno: radionica skiciranja — 3 ideje za izabrani prostor. Korisnik crta ili diktira ravnopravno; skicirnica na ovom sajtu radi i na telefonu.Students and members together: a sketching workshop — 3 ideas for the chosen space. The user draws or dictates as an equal; the sketchpad on this site works on a phone too.

    Predaja: 3 skice sa po jednom rečenicom: kome još ovo pomaže?Deliverable: 3 sketches, each with one sentence: who else does this help?

    Predaj za nedelju 4Submit for week 4

  5. Nedelja 5 — RazradiWeek 5 — Develop

    Cilj: jedan koncept u razmeri, proveren sa korisnikom pre crtanja finala.Goal: one concept at scale, checked with the user before the final drawing.

    Studenti: razrada izabranog koncepta (osnova, presek, detalj). Svaka odluka nosi odgovor na: da li ovo služi svima, ili pravi poseban ulaz „za invalide"?Students: develop the chosen concept (plan, section, detail). Every decision answers: does this serve everyone, or does it create a separate "disabled entrance"?

    Članovi udruženja: recenzent si — koncept koji ne prođe tvoju proveru vraća se na doradu.Association members: you are the reviewer — a concept that fails your check goes back for rework.

    Predaja: crteži + kratak opis rešenja.Deliverable: drawings + a short description of the solution.

    Predaj za nedelju 5Submit for week 5

  6. Nedelja 6 — PokažiWeek 6 — Present

    Cilj: prezentacija pred korisnicima; korisnost ocenjuju oni, ne žiri.Goal: presentation before the users; usefulness is judged by them, not a jury.

    Studenti: finalni pano + refleksija: šta si naučio što nijedna knjiga ne bi rekla?Students: final board + reflection: what did you learn that no book would tell you?

    Članovi udruženja: ocena po jednom pitanju: da li bi ovo stvarno koristio?Association members: one-question verdict: would you actually use this?

    Predaja: pano (slika) + refleksija (tekst).Deliverable: board (image) + reflection (text).

SkicirnicaSketchpad

Nacrtaj ideju prstom ili mišem, preuzmi je kao sliku, pa je priloži uz ideju ili predaju (prevuci sliku u polje za tekst). Skica nije obavezna — tekst vredi jednako. Draw an idea with a finger or mouse, download it as an image, then attach it to an idea or submission (drag the image into the text field). A sketch is optional — text counts just as much.

Pristupačnost ovog sajtaAccessibility of this site

Platforma o univerzalnom dizajnu mora i sama biti univerzalna: semantički HTML, kontrast AA, pun rad sa tastature, vidljiv fokus, poštovanje smanjenog kretanja, bez ijedne spoljne biblioteke. Skicirnica ima ravnopravnu tekstualnu alternativu. Nađeš li barijeru — to je ideja za tablu. A platform about universal design must be universal itself: semantic HTML, AA contrast, full keyboard operation, visible focus, reduced-motion respected, zero external libraries. The sketchpad has an equal text alternative. If you find a barrier — that is an idea for the board.
